Six seasonal skins for the Warm-Market system, drawn from the festive calendar — Independence Day, Kwahu Easter, Eid, Homowo, Hogbetsotso Za and Bronya. Each is one body class: every token, gradient and particle recolors with the season. Tap a festival.

  • Eid follows the lunar calendar, so it's flagged server-side rather than by date math
  • Off-season, the calendar leans toward the next festival coming up
